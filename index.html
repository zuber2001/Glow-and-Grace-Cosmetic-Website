Our Story

India's Most Loved Cosmetic Brand. Estd. 2026,

Glow & Grace began with a simple frustration — makeup that promised "natural" but felt heavy by noon. We started mixing our own formulas, testing on friends and family across Surat.

We're a brand-new, small-batch label — every product tested on real skin, every shade chosen to actually match Indian skin tones, not just imported swatches. Founded in 2026, growing one honest review at a time.
